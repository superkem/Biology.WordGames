How points work

Word search

  • Each word found: Easy 4, Medium 6, Hard 8
  • All words found with no reveals: bonus of 10, 15 or 20
  • Reveal one word from its clue: −3, and that word earns no points
  • Show the whole word list: −20 (once per grid)
  • Hint: −2

Crossword

  • Each word finished: Short 6, Standard 7, Long 8
  • Whole puzzle finished: bonus of 15% of the word points
  • Check that finds a wrong letter: −1
  • Hint: −2

Scramble

  • Each word: Easy 8, Medium 12, Hard 16
  • Each wrong try takes 1 point off that word
  • Hint: −3

Hangman

  • Each word: Easy 8, Medium 12, Hard 16
  • Plus 1 for every wrong guess you did not use
  • Clue hidden: +3 a word
  • Hint: −3

A middle-level game with no help is worth about 100 points. A clue that starts with "Tricky:" gives 2 extra points when you solve it. Points can go below zero, so careless guessing and too many reveals can leave you with a negative score.

Every question scores once. A question you solved, revealed or had shown never comes back, so each new puzzle brings new questions. Word search and crossword can use the same answer again, but only with a different clue. Scramble and hangman never repeat a word. When the questions run out, use Reset points and choose to erase the used questions too. Reset points asks you to confirm twice.
